Privacy Policy — Wataridori

Last updated: 19 September 2026

In short

Wataridori does not collect your information. Everything you record is stored only on your iPhone.

The developer cannot see it. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party SDK.

There is exactly one outside connection: fetching the exchange rate, explained in section 3.

1. What is stored, and where

The app handles only what you enter yourself:

  • Income and spending records (amount, date, category, note, whether it was cash)
  • Job names, recurring-item rules, savings goals, deadlines (visa expiry, flight home and so on), the work log
  • Your pay-period settings, currency and rate, and app settings

All of it is saved in a file on your device. There is no developer server; nothing is uploaded, synced or backed up for you.

If you lose the device or delete the app, the data is gone. Use Backup in Settings to export a file and keep it yourself.

2. What the developer receives

Nothing. There is no account or login, and the developer receives nothing that identifies you.

The app contains no crash or usage reporting.

Apple provides developers with aggregate App Store statistics (such as download counts). These are anonymous and cannot identify an individual.

3. Fetching the exchange rate (the only outside connection)

To show amounts in yen, the app contacts Frankfurter (api.frankfurter.dev), a free exchange-rate service

based on European Central Bank reference rates, at most once a day.

  • What is sent: a single base currency code (for example AUD). Nothing else.
  • What is never sent: your records, amounts, balances, goals, deadlines — no data from inside the app.
  • As with any internet connection, the service can see the connecting IP address. This is the same as opening a website; the app does not store or use it.
  • The connection is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • No request is made while offline, or when the rate has already been fetched that day.

If your everyday currency is set to Japanese yen, no conversion is needed and this connection is not made at all.

4. Device features the app uses

  • Notifications: to remind you the day before a recurring item and about deadlines. Reminders are built entirely on the device and never sent anywhere. The app works without them.
  • Face ID / Touch ID / passcode (App Lock): to confirm it is you when opening the app. Biometric data is handled by iOS and never reaches the app; the app learns only whether the check succeeded.
  • Exporting and importing files: only when you choose to, and only to the location you pick.

5. External links

The app contains no links to external websites.

The app does not assess tax or visa requirements. It only shows dates you entered and what it calculates from them.

6. Children

The app is not aimed at any particular age group and, because it collects no personal information, collects none from children either.

7. Sharing and selling

None. Nothing is collected, so nothing can be shared or sold.

8. Your rights

Your data is on your device and entirely under your control. You can edit or delete records at any time,

and deleting the app removes everything. There is no need to ask the developer for deletion — the developer holds nothing.

9. Changes to this policy

When it changes, the date at the top is updated. Any change to how data is stored or transmitted is also noted in the app's release notes.
