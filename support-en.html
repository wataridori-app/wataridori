Before you write

  • My records disappeared — Records live only on your device. Resetting the device or deleting the app loses them. Export a file from Backup in Settings and keep it safe.
  • No yen amounts — The rate is fetched once a day. It is not shown while offline or before the first fetch. When your everyday currency is yen there is nothing to convert, so nothing is shown.
  • Reminders don't arrive — In the iPhone Settings app, open Notifications and check that Wataridori is allowed.
  • My pay periods don't line up — In Settings, open Pay period and change the interval and anchor (your most recent payday). You can see the new boundaries before saving.
